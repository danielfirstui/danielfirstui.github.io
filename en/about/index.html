About transportoradea.com

Our story

transportoradea.com is a local, family-run private transfer service from Oradea, Bihor county. We started with a simple idea: the journey to the airport should be the easy part of flying — a price known in advance, a punctual driver and a clean, comfortable car.

Over the years, the Oradea – Budapest Airport route has become our speciality, complemented by trips to Debrecen, Timișoara and Cluj-Napoca. We know every border crossing, every terminal and every alternative road — and you can feel it in every journey.

Your driver

At the wheel you will have a professional driver with 25 years of experience, who has covered tens of thousands of kilometres on the routes between Romania and Hungary. He speaks fluent Romanian, Hungarian, English and Dutch, so communication is clear for every passenger, wherever they come from.

Punctuality is the ground rule: for airport pickups we track your flight in real time and adjust the pickup time automatically, at no extra cost in case of delay.

The car

You travel in a 2019 Škoda Octavia estate: dual-zone Climatronic air conditioning, automatic transmission, a clean interior and a generous boot — room for 4 passengers and 4 large suitcases, plus carry-on bags.

The car is regularly inspected and maintained, and passenger comfort and safety come first on every trip, day or night.

How we work

Bookings are made exclusively on WhatsApp: send us your date, time and destination, and we confirm within minutes, in writing, the fixed price and the trip details. The confirmed price is the price you pay — no meter, no surge pricing, no surprises.
