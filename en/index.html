Our Private Transfer Service in Oradea

We are a local private transfer company based in Oradea, Bihor county, specialising in door-to-door rides to airports and cities across western Romania and eastern Hungary. Our most requested route is the Oradea to Budapest Airport transfer, and we also run daily trips to Debrecen, Timișoara and Cluj-Napoca — in both directions.

We serve all of Bihor county — Oradea, Beiuș, Marghita, Salonta, Aleșd and the surrounding villages — picking you up at any address, at any hour. You travel in a 2019 Škoda Octavia estate with dual-zone Climatronic air conditioning, automatic transmission and a boot that easily fits 4 large suitcases.

Your driver has 25 years of professional experience and speaks Romanian, Hungarian, English and Dutch — clear communication for every passenger. For airport pickups we track your flight in real time: land early or late, we adjust the pickup time at no extra cost. Read the full story of the driver and the car on our About page.

The price is always fixed and confirmed in writing on WhatsApp before the trip: 180 EUR for Budapest, 60 EUR for Debrecen, 120 EUR for Timișoara or Cluj — for the whole car, not per person. No meter, no surge pricing, no surprises at the end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about private transfer from Oradea

How much does the transfer from Oradea to Budapest Airport cost?

Private transfer from Oradea to Budapest Liszt Ferenc Airport costs 180 EUR for 1-4 passengers. The price is fixed and includes all fees, regardless of traffic or delays.

How many people fit in the car and how much luggage can I bring?

The Škoda Octavia 2019 estate comfortably fits up to 4 passengers. The spacious trunk has enough room for 4 large suitcases and carry-on bags.

What languages does the driver speak?

The driver is a professional with 25 years of experience and speaks English, Dutch, Romanian and Hungarian fluently. Communication is clear with no language barriers.

Can I pay by card or only cash?

We accept cash payment in EUR or RON. For other payment methods, please contact us on WhatsApp before the trip.

How long does the trip from Oradea to Budapest take?

The journey from Oradea to Budapest Airport takes approximately 2.5-3 hours, depending on traffic and border crossing wait times. We monitor road conditions in real time.

Do you offer transfers to Debrecen Airport?

Yes, we offer private transfer from Oradea to Debrecen Airport at a fixed price of 60 EUR for 1-4 passengers. The journey takes approximately 1 hour.
