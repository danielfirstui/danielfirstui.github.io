About the Budapest – Oradea route

A well-organised transfer starts before you land. We take your flight details, track the real arrival time and are at the terminal exactly when you walk out — nothing to find, and nothing extra to pay if your flight is delayed. From the baggage belt to your front door in Oradea, the journey continues without interruptions.

The roughly 230 km drive crosses the border at Borș and takes between 2.5 and 3 hours, depending on traffic and border queues. It is the comfortable alternative to trains with changes or bus combinations — especially after a long flight, with luggage and children.

On request, we can make a short stop along the way — petrol station, supermarket or a few minutes' rest. Mention it when booking and we will include it in the plan at no extra cost.

Since 1 January 2025, Romania has been a full Schengen member and there is no longer systematic control at the land border with Hungary — at Borș you usually drive straight through, which makes the Budapest–Oradea journey more predictable than ever. All you need with you is a valid ID document (ID card or passport). In peak periods — the start and end of holidays, public holidays — the driver knows the busy hours and can recommend the best departure time. Please book at least one day in advance, especially for late arrivals; for urgent same-day trips, message us and we will check availability immediately.

Pickup at Budapest Airport (BUD)

The meeting point is the arrivals area of your terminal (2A or 2B) — we confirm the exact spot on WhatsApp before you land. Your driver waits with a name board and helps with luggage to the car. For the outbound trip, see the Oradea → Budapest page.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the transfer from Budapest to Oradea cost?

The fixed price is 180 EUR for the whole car, 1–4 passengers. It includes all fees and border waiting time — no surprises.

How long does the trip from Budapest to Oradea take?

Approximately 2.5–3 hours, depending on traffic and waiting time at the Borș border.
