About the Cluj-Napoca – Oradea route

Cluj airport serves flights from all over Europe, and many travellers from Bihor choose to fly from here. After landing, your driver waits in the arrivals area — nothing to search for and no dependence on train schedules. The roughly 150 km to Oradea take about 2 hours.

The route is perfect for passengers on Cluj flights who live in Oradea and Bihor, for students coming home and for business travellers on a tight schedule. The car is dedicated exclusively to your group — no waiting for other passengers, no detours.

The fixed price of 120 EUR covers the whole car, for 1–4 passengers, with all fees included. You pay in cash at the end of the trip, in EUR or RON — exactly the amount confirmed on WhatsApp at booking.

Avram Iancu Airport is compact and fast: from the terminal exit to the car it is just a few steps, and within 10 minutes of pickup we are already on the road to Oradea. Many passengers living in Bihor choose flights from Cluj for better prices and schedules — and a private transfer completes the trip perfectly, without a night lost in Cluj or catching the first morning train. If you have a long layover or arrive late, we operate 24/7. Book early on weekends and during festival season, when demand on this route rises sharply.

Pickup at Avram Iancu Airport (CLJ)

We meet in the arrivals area, at the exit from baggage claim, holding a board with your name — we confirm the exact spot on WhatsApp before you land. We track your flight in real time, so if you are delayed, we adjust the pickup for free. For the outbound trip, see the Oradea → Cluj page.
