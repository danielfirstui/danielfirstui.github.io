About the Debrecen – Oradea route

Debrecen Airport is Bihor's low-cost gateway: Wizz Air flights to destinations across Europe, just 60 km from Oradea. After landing, a private transfer is the fastest way home — no rare buses, no taxis refusing cross-border trips.

We track your flight in real time. Land early or late, the driver adjusts the pickup time automatically at no extra cost. We meet in the arrivals area, holding a board with your name.

Low-cost flights often arrive late at night. We operate 24/7 and the price stays the same whatever the hour: 60 EUR fixed for the whole car, 1–4 passengers.

Late arrivals are this route's speciality: many low-cost flights land in Debrecen after midnight, when there is no public transport towards Romania. We operate around the clock, and the price stays 60 EUR whatever the hour. Since Romania joined Schengen, you usually drive through Borș without stopping, so about an hour after leaving the terminal you are home. You pay cash at the end, in EUR or RON — useful to know if you did not manage to exchange money. We recommend booking at least one day ahead; send us your flight number too, so we can track it in real time.

We can drop you off not only in Oradea but anywhere in Bihor — Beiuș, Marghita, Salonta or Aleșd — at the same fixed price; just confirm the address when booking. Do not forget a valid ID document (ID card or passport): although the border is crossed without stopping, the document is mandatory on international trips. Send us your Wizz Air flight number at booking — we track it in real time, and if the arrival time changes, the driver adjusts the pickup without you having to do anything.

Pickup at Debrecen Airport (DEB)

Debrecen Airport has a single compact terminal — the arrivals exit is easy to find, and your driver waits there with your name board. We confirm the exact meeting point on WhatsApp before you land. For the outbound trip, see the Oradea → Debrecen page.
