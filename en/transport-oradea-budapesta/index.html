About the Oradea – Budapest route

The roughly 230 km journey crosses the border at Borș and usually takes between 2.5 and 3 hours, depending on traffic and border waiting times. We monitor road and border conditions in real time, and for flights we calculate the departure time so that you reach the terminal at least 2 hours before boarding.

This route is ideal for international flights from Budapest (Wizz Air, Ryanair, Lufthansa, Turkish Airlines and others), for families with lots of luggage and for business travellers who want a calm ride without train or bus changes. Compared to driving yourself, you save multi-day airport parking fees and the tiring drive back.

Whether you search for an Oradea airport transfer, an airport shuttle or a private car service to Budapest, the service is the same: a dedicated car, door to door, at one fixed price — 180 EUR for the whole car.

Pickup & drop-off at Budapest Airport (BUD)

For departures, we drop you directly at the correct terminal for your flight — 2A or 2B — so you never haul luggage across long distances. For arrivals, on the return Budapest → Oradea trip, we wait in the arrivals hall with a name board and track your flight in real time: if it is delayed, we adjust the pickup at no extra cost.

What the transfer includes

  • Pickup at your address in Oradea or anywhere in Bihor county, at your chosen time

Frequently asked questions

How much does the transfer from Oradea to Budapest Airport cost?

The price is fixed: 180 EUR for the whole car (1–4 passengers). It includes all fees, fuel and border waiting time — no extra costs.

How long does the trip from Oradea to Budapest take?

Approximately 2.5–3 hours, depending on traffic and waiting time at the Borș border crossing. We recommend leaving at least 5 hours before your departure time.

Do you pick me up at my address and drop me at the terminal?

Yes — the service is door to door. We pick you up at the address you give us in Oradea or the surroundings and drop you directly at the right terminal for your flight.

What is the difference versus a taxi or rideshare?
