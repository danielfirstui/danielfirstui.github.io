About the Oradea – Cluj-Napoca route

The roughly 150 km between Oradea and Cluj cross Țara Călatei, one of the most beautiful regions of Transylvania — the drive takes around 2 hours. The route is popular with students, families and business travellers, as well as passengers flying from Avram Iancu Airport, Romania's second largest airport.

A private transfer is the direct alternative to train or bus: you leave at the time that suits you, get picked up at your door and arrive exactly at your destination — the departures terminal, the campus, your hotel or a company office. No changes, no luggage carried through stations.

Whether you look for transport or a transfer from Oradea to Cluj, the service is the same: a car dedicated to your group, a driver with 25 years of experience and a fixed price of 120 EUR, confirmed in writing before the trip.

The Oradea – Cluj route is in high demand during peak periods: the start and end of the university year, the big Cluj festivals (such as UNTOLD or Electric Castle in Bonțida) and long weekends. During these times we recommend booking a few days in advance. On Sunday evenings and Monday mornings traffic towards Cluj is heavier, so for flights we leave earlier. If you are travelling for an event with a fixed start time, tell us when you need to arrive and we will plan the drive with a time buffer, so you get there stress-free.

Transfer to Avram Iancu Airport (CLJ)

For flights from Cluj, we calculate the departure time from Oradea so you reach the terminal at least 2 hours before boarding. We drop you right at the departures entrance. For the return Cluj → Oradea trip, we wait at arrivals and track your flight in real time.
