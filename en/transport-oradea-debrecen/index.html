About the Oradea – Debrecen route

For a low-cost flight from Debrecen, every minute counts. A private transfer saves you expensive parking, taxis that avoid cross-border trips and the fixed timetables of public transport. We leave Oradea at the right time, cross the Borș border and you arrive relaxed at the terminal, at least 2 hours before departure.

Wizz Air flights from Debrecen Airport often leave at unusual hours — very early morning or late at night. We operate 24/7, so we adapt our schedule to your flight time at no extra cost for night trips.

At 60 EUR for the whole car, a private transfer is often cheaper than several days of airport parking — and certainly more comfortable. The price is fixed and confirmed on WhatsApp before the trip.

Since Romania joined Schengen (January 2025), the land border with Hungary is crossed without systematic control — at Borș there is rarely any waiting, which makes the one-hour trip even more predictable. Debrecen Airport is an excellent alternative to Budapest for certain Wizz Air low-cost destinations (for example London), with much smaller parking fees and queues. If you have an early-morning flight, we recommend leaving Oradea 3.5–4 hours ahead; we will tell you the exact optimal time at booking, depending on the day of the week. Booking one day in advance is ideal, but we also check urgent same-day requests.

Transfer to Debrecen Airport (DEB)

Debrecen Airport is compact and easy to navigate — we drop you right at the departures entrance. For the return Debrecen → Oradea trip, we wait at arrivals and track your flight in real time, at no extra cost if you are delayed.
