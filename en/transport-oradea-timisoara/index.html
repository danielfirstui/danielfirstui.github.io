About the Oradea – Timișoara route

The roughly 160 km between Oradea and Timișoara take about 2.5 hours, on the direct route via Arad. Our driver, with 25 years of experience, drives this route regularly and knows every option for avoiding traffic.

A private transfer gives you what the train cannot: departure at the time that suits you, pickup at your door and a direct ride to the airport or any address in Timișoara. No changes, no waiting in stations, no luggage carried across platforms.

The route is especially popular with passengers flying from Traian Vuia, with students and with business travellers between Bihor and Banat. The price is fixed: 120 EUR for the whole car, confirmed in writing on WhatsApp before the trip.

The route via Arad is the most direct and fastest road between Oradea and Timișoara: DN79 to Arad, then on the bypass towards Timișoara. Traian Vuia Airport is about 12 km from the city centre, so if you have errands in the city before or after the airport, we can combine the stops in the same trip. The route is used intensively by business people with meetings in Banat and by students at Timișoara's universities. For morning flights, leaving Oradea around 4–5 am gets you to the terminal comfortably. Book one day ahead for the exact time you want.

Transfer to Traian Vuia Airport (TSR)

For flights, we calculate the departure time from Oradea so you reach the terminal at least 2 hours before boarding. We drop you right at the departures terminal, and on the way back — the Timișoara → Oradea trip — we wait at arrivals with flight tracking included.
