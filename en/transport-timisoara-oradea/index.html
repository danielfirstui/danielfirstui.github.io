About the Timișoara – Oradea route

After a flight, the last thing you want is to wait for a train with changes. Your driver waits in the arrivals area of Traian Vuia Airport, tracks your flight in real time and adjusts the pickup if you are delayed — at no extra cost.

The roughly 160 km drive, via Arad, takes about 2.5 hours. The car is dedicated exclusively to your group: no waiting for other passengers, no detours, and you stop whenever you like.

The price is fixed: 120 EUR for the whole car, 1–4 passengers, all fees included. You pay in cash at the end, in EUR or RON — exactly the amount confirmed on WhatsApp at booking.

At Traian Vuia arrivals, leaving the terminal takes only a few minutes — the airport is small and efficient, and the driver waits right at the exit of the arrivals area. In about 2.5 hours you are in Oradea, without depending on trains that require a change in Arad. If you land late in the evening, that is no problem: we operate 24/7 at the same price. Many clients use this route for connections to Bihor from Western European flights landing in Timișoara. Send us your flight number at booking and we take care of the rest — including adjusting the pickup time if your flight is delayed.

We can also pick you up in Arad or in towns along the road to Oradea — mention the address at booking and you will receive the final price in writing, with no surprises. At your destination, we drop you at any address in Oradea or in the towns of Bihor, at the same fixed price. For families with children or passengers with lots of luggage, the estate car offers space and comfort for the whole 2.5-hour drive. You pay at the end, in cash, in EUR or RON — exactly the amount confirmed on WhatsApp.

Pickup at Traian Vuia Airport (TSR)

We meet in the arrivals area, at the exit from baggage claim, holding a board with your name — we confirm the exact spot on WhatsApp before you land. We also pick up from any address in Timișoara (hotel, home, office) at the same price. For the outbound trip, see the Oradea → Timișoara page.
